# InfraGuard AI — Judge Demo Script

A citizen sees a dangerous pothole.

1. Open InfraGuard AI — the citizen home interface is the supplied final design.
2. Tap **Report an Issue**.
3. Grant camera, microphone and location permission on first use.
4. Take a real pothole photo.
5. InfraGuard automatically attaches GPS and hides detected people / plates / QR codes.
6. Voice starts automatically.
7. Speak in Telugu, Hindi, Tamil or another supported Indian language.
8. Saaras converts it directly to English.
9. Sarvam-105B identifies:
   **Road Pothole / High / Roads Department**
10. Submit.
11. Show duplicate detection and priority score.
12. Open Officer Console.
13. Move case through Assigned -> In Progress -> Resolved and upload field evidence.
14. Return to My Reports / Notifications.
15. Open Analytics.

Closing line:
**InfraGuard AI turns one citizen photo and one natural-language voice report into
privacy-aware, location-aware, accountable city action.**
